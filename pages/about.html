New Moon Motor Bakery & Photography Company

Currently, a sourdough, poolish, banana bread, and pumpkin bread are on offer.
New Moon Motor Bakery & Photography Company is the project of Chris Quinnson, from Colorado Springs, Colorado. Quinnson's French-school, high-altitude baking seeks to find excellence in the four simple, high-quality ingredients of water, flour, salt, & yeast. It is informed by the book of the same name by Ken Forkish, whose excellent writing, exquisite knowledge, and detailed explainations are to credit for the flavors of the bread. King Arthur Baking Company is also to credit via their two excellent books, _____ and _____. Quinnson's photographic style has been inspired by the photography of Paulie B., Reubin Radding, Trevor Wisecup, and Mark Portillo.
